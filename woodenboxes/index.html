For many years Russian craftsmen kept learning to make use of wood, discover its properties, discern different patterns of wood. Our ancestors amazed the world with wooden churches and chambers, plates and dishes, furniture, self-propelled carts, sawing machines, bicycles and even ... pocket watches made of nothing but wood. Some of these things stayed in legends, some became part of museum expositions. They appeared to be unclaimed by modern life.

However, elaborate sets of chess, snuff-boxes, smoking pipes, various caskets which were popular not only with our great grand mothers and fathers but were readily purchased by merchants from England, France, Germany and America, have survived as goods until the present time. They have been constantly improved by the craftsmen talent.
