Dear Subscribers,

Recently we have attended National Art Materials Trade Association (NAMTA)
trade show in Chicago as an exhibitor:
http://www.kolonok.com/klnk_at_namta.html
This certainly was a very exciting event. We came back with new ideas.
Changes are coming - please, stay tuned.

One of the news is that we are starting to carry Martin/F. Weber Winter 
Harvest Kolinsky Brushes. Martin/F. Weber is the oldest manufacturer
of art materials in the United States:
http://www.weberart.com/about.html

For a quite some time we realized that we need not only high-end Kolonok 
Kolinsky sable brushes but the best of the high-end Kolinsky brushes as well. 
We tested Winter Harvest Kolinsky brushes with our friends from Martin/F. Weber 
and fell in love with these brushes.

Martin/F. Weber is currently in the process of developing Winter Harvest 
Kolinsky brushes line. Thoroughly testing brushes, rounds up to # 10 are now 
manufactured. Within the next year rounds up to # 16 should be launched. 
In our opinion Winter Harvest Kolinsky Brushes surpass both W&N series 9 and 
Isabey Kolinskies (this was exactly the purpose of launching Winter Harvest 
Kolinsky line). They even surpass Kolonok Kolinskies :) We have agreed to carry 
Winter Harvest Kolinsky series and placed initial order. Martin/F. Weber has 
officially allowed us to become "the oldest retailer of Winter Harvest Kolinsky 
series". All the buzz about this series will probably start in about one year.

The good news is that we managed to get two brushes: round # 7 and round # 9 
right from the trade show. In the spirit of "the oldest retailer" these will be 
the first Winter Harvest Kolinsky brushes sold anywhere in the world.

In summary, these are Winter harvest, Tip of the tail, 100% Male hair brushes
with unique hair dressing which allows unmatched control of the paint flow. 
To read more and place order, please, visit
http://www.kolinskyartbrushes.com/winter_harvest.html

We expect the full (so far up to round # 10) line to be available at
http://www.kolinskyartbrushes.com
in about two months. Good luck!
