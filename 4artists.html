Artists FAQ: 
   

Is there any of Kolinsky stores out in cities? 
No, we are purely internet retailer. Our inventory is located in Durham, NC. 
 

Are the ferrules of your brushes nickel (aluminium) plated or solid nickel (aluminium)? 
The ferrules are nickel (aluminium) plated. They are imported by our manufacturer from Japan. 
 

Seamless or not?
They are seamless!
 

How is the balancing of the brushes? 
They are well balanced. 
 

Why synthetics is Japanese? 
Our manufacturer imports synthetics hair from Japan. Natural types of hair are from Russia. 
 

Please tell me the shipping options to the USA/Canada/Austria/etc. 
Within the USA we ship by First Class Mail. We charge $3.95 for S&H. Our actual expenses are larger (we always use mailing tubes to ship our orders). Shipping to Canada also costs $3.95, but we ship to Canada by Air Mail. Shipping to any other country will cost $4.95 and is by Air Mail. 
 

I'm currious... . Where are you shipping from? 
We are shipping from Durham, NC, USA 
 

Please, send a sample of the sable brushes to ... . 
We are sorry, at this moment of our business development we can not afford to give away any samples. 
 

Exactly how big is this brush? 
The sizes of each particular art brush are given on our web site. To get sizes in inches, please, use our Millimeter To Inch Converter  
 

Please tell me what currency your prices are? U.S. dollar? 
Yes, they are in US dollars. 
 

Do you have a catalog  you can send me? 
Sometimes we have few catalogues and we ship them upon request. But most of the time we are out of them. Please, surf our web site for the full and up-to-date information. All the brushes we have are on the web site. 
 

Can I order custom art brushes? 
Sure, our manufacturer can produce any types of art brushes. But be aware that it could take two or three months and can be quite expensive. If you know exactly what you need, please, feel free to request a quote.
