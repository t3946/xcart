About Kolonok™ trademark

- Kolonok™ stands for Kolinsky Sable in Russian. Since we import art brushes from Russia - this trademark is very natural.
- "Kolo" is a slightly changed root of "Color" which is very relevant to the art materials industry.
- Three drops of paint above Kolonok™ text symbolize both Art Materials industry and Russia. They remind us of the domes of Russian Orthodox Christian Temple.
- Gold letters on the Black background symbolize highest quality of our products. Please, see the Section about our manufacturer.
