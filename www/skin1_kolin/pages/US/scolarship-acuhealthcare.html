S3 Fellowship Award for Medical, Nursing & Pharmacy Students

 

Award Amount: $1,000 annually, non-renewable.

Recipients: One award offered annually.

Eligibility:

  • Must be a full-time student.

  • Must be enrolled in a medical, nursing or pharmacy school during the academic year following the deadline date.

  • Must be pursuing a degree in healthcare, medicine or a related field.

  • Must be enrolled in medical schools in the U.S. and Canada.

Additional Information:

  • The winner will be selected from the submitted essays.

  • Past scholarship recipients are allowed to reapply.

 

Deadline: Sunday, April 1, 2018.  A decision will be announced May 1, 2018.

Deadline Information:

  • Students must first complete the application form and send an essay of 1-2 pages on the topic of ‘The Most Important Medical Invention’.

  • The winner will be required to submit an email, including the following documentation: a recent resume or CV, an enrollment verification letter, two letters of recommendation.

Contact information: fellowship@s3stores.com
