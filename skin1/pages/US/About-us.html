Kolonok, Inc. is a Canadian company (incorporated in Ontario) created to distribute the finest quality art supplies in Canada, USA, and worldwide. It was originally formed in 1999 in the United States and in July of 2005 moved to Canada.

Direct connection with our manufacturers in Russia and worldwide on a day-to-day basis coupled with our experience and attention to details allows us to offer the highest quality products at low prices as well as ultimate shopping convenience.
