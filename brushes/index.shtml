Kolonok Professional Artist's Brushes

Main characteristics of the artists brushes
  • ability to form a sharp tip (to precisely place the paint)
  • elasticity of the hair (to recover its original shape after a stroke)
  • ability to control a paint flow (to hold and dispense paint predictably)

    How to distinguish art brush  Every Kolonok brush has Kolonok lettering on it or/and our manufacturer's golden squirrel logo and a number which corresponds to the diameter / width of the hair tuft for a round / flat brush.
     
    Types of artists brushes  We offer Professional Kolinsky Sable, Siberian Squirrel, and Japanese Synthetics artist brushes. We also offer Technical Bleached Bristle art brushes. When placing the order, please, take into account the last but one column: the number of the artists brushes in stock.
