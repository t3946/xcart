Technical Bleached Bristle Art Brushes
 
 
Bristles are very resilient and have "flags" (split ends at the tips) to hold a great volume of heavy material. Each flag has a needle ended taper for excellent control of paint. Bristle brushes are ideally suited for heavy bodied paints like oils and acrylics.
