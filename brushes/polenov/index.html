History
Since the creation of our brush business in 1999 we actively searched for the finest Kolinsky sable art brushes on the market. We were also directly contacted by many brush makers over the years. They were from all over the world. We carefully examined and tested many Kolinsky sable art brushes against each other. Polenov Male Tail Kolinsky brushes is the outcome of our long search for perfection.

Hair Quality and Arrangement
Most manufacturers do not have access to the finest Kolinsky sable hair these days. Unfortunately price is a decisive factor. If manufacturer buys the best hair – the price of the brush quickly goes up, hence the demand drops. Besides, expensive hair requires special handling and attention. Thus, expensive Kolinsky sable brush lines do not bring "big players" enough business for all the hassle involved. That is probably why the company we found is a small brush making company which is not located in China and not well-known at all. They are perfectionists in every aspect of brush making. They buy the best male tail Kolinsky hair harvested in January. It has unbelievable spring and allows them to go up to HUGE brush sizes. Their largest round # 20 brush has the hair tuft length of 38 mm or 1·½ inches. This is truly amazing! Please, look at our competitors' Kolinsky sable brush lines to compare. If manufacturer can not dress large rounds then it probably does not have access to the finest male tail Kolinsky hair.
• Note. Significant fraction of the hair tuft goes under the ferrule to make the hair tuft stable. For large Kolinsky sable brushes every additional mm means a lot!

The Price
As anything of superior quality these brushes do not come cheap. In fact, the initial pricing we got from the manufacturer was so high that we thought it would be impossible to do any business. Luckily we were able to negotiate a better deal. Our customers are guaranteed to get the best price on the market because we will not charge you for the brand (we are not going to run expensive advertising of Polenov Kolinsky brushes because these are limited edition brushes) and there is no middleman involved.

Why Your Brush Quantities are So Low?
These are very expensive brushes. So far we placed an order for small quantity. As our sales take off these brushes will be re-stocked. Please, bear with us ;)
